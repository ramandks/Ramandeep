Data & Analytics

Data & Analytics. Answers, not reconciliation.

Unify your data, surface what matters and put decision-ready insight in front of the right people.

Book a Demo See our solutions

What Data & Analytics covers

Six capabilities we deploy most often. Every engagement starts with the subset that pays back first.

Unified data layer

Pull sales, service, finance and product data into one modelled, trustworthy source.

Dashboards people use

Role-specific views that answer the questions each team actually asks, not a wall of every metric available.

Ask in plain English

Natural-language querying over governed data, so a manager can ask a question without waiting on an analyst.

Forecasting & scoring

Demand forecasts, churn risk and lead scoring wired back into the systems where people act.

Alerts, not reports

Anomaly detection that tells you when something moved, instead of a PDF nobody opens.

Governed access

Row-level permissions, lineage and definitions your finance team will sign off on.

How we deliver it

A four-stage path from first conversation to a production system your team owns.

01

Agree the questions

We start from the decisions you need to make, then work backwards to the metrics and sources required.

02

Model the data

Sources are connected, cleaned and modelled with documented definitions so numbers reconcile across teams.

03

Build the surfaces

Dashboards, alerts and natural-language access, each tied to a named owner and decision.

04

Close the loop

Insight is pushed back into CRM, support and ops tools, so the finding becomes an action automatically.

What you get

Outcomes, not experiments

Each deployment ships with the things that make it survivable after go-live: monitoring, documentation, an owner and a measured baseline.

  • A measured before-and-after on the process we automated
  • Dashboards for volume, failure rate and hours returned
  • Documented runbooks and handover to your team
  • Security review, access controls and audit logging
  • A roadmap for the next two use cases
