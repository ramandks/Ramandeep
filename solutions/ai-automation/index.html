AI Automation

AI Automation. Give your team back the week.

End-to-end workflow automation that connects your apps, moves your data and removes the busywork.

Book a Demo See our solutions

What AI Automation covers

Six capabilities we deploy most often. Every engagement starts with the subset that pays back first.

End-to-end workflows

Chain triggers, conditions, approvals and system writes into one reliable process that runs without supervision.

Document processing

Invoices, contracts, application forms and PDFs read, validated and pushed into the right system of record.

Data sync & hygiene

Keep CRM, ERP, finance and support tools consistent, with de-duplication and conflict rules you define.

Routing & assignment

Score, prioritise and assign work to the right person or queue using your rules plus model judgement.

Notifications that matter

Alert the right people at the right moment — and stay quiet the rest of the time.

Monitoring & retries

Failure handling, retries with backoff, dead-letter queues and alerting so a broken step never silently drops work.

How we deliver it

A four-stage path from first conversation to a production system your team owns.

01

Find the money

We time the current process, count the touches and rank candidates by hours saved against build effort.

02

Design the happy path

Model the standard flow first, then enumerate the exceptions — that list is where most automations quietly fail.

03

Build and shadow-run

The automation runs alongside the manual process, so you can compare outputs before anyone relies on it.

04

Cut over and monitor

Switch on with alerting and a rollback path. You get a dashboard of runs, failures and hours saved.

What you get

Outcomes, not experiments

Each deployment ships with the things that make it survivable after go-live: monitoring, documentation, an owner and a measured baseline.

  • A measured before-and-after on the process we automated
  • Dashboards for volume, failure rate and hours returned
  • Documented runbooks and handover to your team
  • Security review, access controls and audit logging
  • A roadmap for the next two use cases

Every step has retry and failure handling. Failed runs go to a queue with the full payload, an alert reaches the owner, and the work can be replayed once fixed. Nothing disappears silently.
